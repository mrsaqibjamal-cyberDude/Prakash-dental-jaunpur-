ABOUT THE HOSPITAL

A hospital-grade standard of dental & facial surgery

Prakash Dental Hospital was built to bring surgical-level maxillofacial and dental care to Jaunpur — under one roof, with one standard of precision. Dr. Prashant treats everything from routine root canals to complex facial trauma and jaw reconstruction.

Every procedure follows strict sterilization protocols, and every patient is walked through their treatment plan in plain language before any work begins.

  • Advanced maxillofacial & facial trauma surgery
  • Dedicated sterile operating suite
  • Open Sundays till 2:00 PM for urgent care
  • 24/7 emergency dental & facial injury response
